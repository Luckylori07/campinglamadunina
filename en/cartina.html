How to find us

From the official map, this is the route shown.

  1. Leave the Milano–Laghi motorway at Vergiate–S. Calende.
  2. Continue towards Corgeno on the road shown on the map (Laveno–Luino expressway).
  3. Reach Varano Borghi: the campsite is at Via dei Martiri 12, near the lake.

The official diagram shows neither distances nor times. Always check with your satnav.
