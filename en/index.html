The campsite

On the lakeshore, among greenery and quiet.

Camping La Madunina is in Varano Borghi, on the shore of Lake Comabbio: an oasis of quiet, suited to peaceful, relaxing stays.

In the middle of the site stands the main building with bar-restaurant, pizzeria, games room, billiards, table tennis and a dance floor. There are also a swimming pool, a children's play area, a football pitch, a bocce court, a cycle path and Wi-Fi throughout the site.

  • On Lake Comabbio, in a green setting
  • Swimming pool: use is included in the displayed rates
  • Monthly, seasonal and winter flat-rate packages

Nature and surroundings

Lake Comabbio, a small lake between Lake Maggiore and the Varese area.

A glacial lake between Lake Maggiore and Lake Varese, with quiet shores, reed beds and water lilies.

  • A compact lakeAbout 3.4 km² of surface and about 9 km of shoreline, shared by five municipalities including Varano Borghi (source: Wikipedia).
  • On foot or by bikeA cycle and walking path of about 12 km runs around the lake (source: Wikipedia). The campsite has a cycle path.
  • Light and waterSunrises, sunsets and reflections of the hills: the lake changes with the seasons.

Descriptions of the area come from public sources. Private access, jetties or organised activities are to be verified with the campsite.
