Frequently asked questions.

How can I contact the campsite?

Phone +39 0332 960361, mobile +39 347 7867227, email info@campinglamadunina.com. You will also find the form on the Contact page.

How do I get to the campsite?

The address is Via dei Martiri 12, 21020 Varano Borghi (VA). The “Getting here” page has the official map and a link for driving directions.

What accommodation is available?

Pitches for tents, motorhomes and caravans, according to tourism portals and the price list (“pitch” item). Bungalows or rental lodging do not appear on the official website.

Where can I see the price list?

The Prices page has the rates and the original PDF. The price list dates from February 2024 and must be confirmed with the campsite.

Are pets allowed?

The price list includes a dog rate. A third-party portal states dogs are allowed on a lead and only on the pitches: please ask the campsite to confirm.

What services are there?

According to the official website: bar, restaurant, pizzeria, games room, swimming pool, children's play area, football pitch, bocce court, cycle path, Wi-Fi, hot showers and washing machine. See the Services page.

How do I ask about a stay?

Call or write to the campsite. This site does not handle online bookings: the demo does not simulate any.

Is the campsite open all year?

The official website says “open all year”, while the price list shows a closure from 7 January to 6 February. Please ask the campsite to confirm.

Are there monthly or seasonal packages?

The website mentions monthly, seasonal or winter packages. The price list mentions six-month and annual agreements and a flat-rate price list on request. Conditions must be asked of the campsite.
